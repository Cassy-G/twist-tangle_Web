A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How long does a custom piece take?+

It depends on the piece, but most custom knits take around 3–6 weeks after the design and materials are agreed. We’ll give you a clear timeline before starting.

Can I choose the colors and fibers?+

Absolutely. We’ll share a small, thoughtful selection of yarn options and help you find a combination that feels right for you.

Do I need experience for a workshop?+

Not at all. Our workshops are intentionally small and welcoming to complete beginners. You’ll leave with a finished project and the confidence to keep going.
